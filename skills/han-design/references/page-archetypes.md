# Page archetypes and starters

Use these full-page starters as composition references. Replace the content, adapt asset paths, and translate markup into the target framework. Do not preserve a starter section merely because it exists.

## Starter routing

| User task | Starter | Default intensity | Useful starting themes |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Brand, service, lifestyle, tea, craft | `assets/starters/brand-landing.html` | 1–2 | `song`, `tea`, `celadon`, `pine-wheat`, `plum-blush` |
| Product launch, technology, device, AI service | `assets/starters/product-launch.html` | 1 | `song`, `ink`, `ocean-orchid`, `caramel-cream` |
| Dashboard, admin, SaaS application | `assets/starters/dashboard-shell.html` | 0–1 | `song`, `ink`, `porcelain`, `ocean-orchid` |
| Museum, archive, gallery, exhibition | `assets/starters/exhibition-page.html` | 1–2 | `song`, `ink`, `weijin`, `porcelain`, `dunhuang` |
| Festival, ceremony, campaign, game launch | `assets/starters/festival-campaign.html` | 2–3 | `vermilion`, `tang`, `wuxia`, `berry-butter` |
| Article, studio journal, documentation, long-form content | `assets/starters/editorial-page.html` | 1 | `song`, `ink`, `pine-wheat`, `caramel-cream` |

## Composition rules

- Brand landing pages need a promise, proof, product or service story, and conversion path.
- Product launch pages need a crisp hero, capability hierarchy, comparison or proof, and a final action.
- Dashboards need familiar navigation, dense but readable information, clear state, and restrained decoration.
- Exhibition pages need chronology, metadata, captions, source-aware context, and practical visitor information.
- Campaign pages may use dramatic composition, but dates, eligibility, registration, and status messaging remain conventional.
- Editorial pages need a readable measure, table of contents or progress cues for long content, and clear related reading.

## Adaptation checklist

- Replace the starter brand, title, body copy, metrics, dates, links, and calls to action.
- Remove sections that do not support the real task.
- Keep at most one primary CTA label per major conversion path.
- Preserve existing application behavior and accessible components.
- Use repository media when available and replace CSS-only visual regions when real imagery materially improves understanding.
- Keep starter-local classes local. Do not add them to Han's core component catalog unless they become reusable across unrelated products.
