# Regional, ethnic, religious, and living contexts

China contains many regional, ethnic, linguistic, religious, craft, and social traditions. Do not treat them as interchangeable substyles of a single generic “Chinese aesthetic.”

## Required checks

When a request names a region, ethnic group, religion, festival, ritual, costume, craft community, or living heritage practice:

1. Confirm the exact community and preferred name.
2. Identify whether the material is historical, contemporary, sacred, ceremonial, commercial, or everyday.
3. Use sources produced by or in consultation with the represented community when available.
4. Separate state, museum, tourism, commercial, and community descriptions when they differ.
5. Avoid turning clothing, sacred objects, writing, or identity markers into generic borders and icons.

## Language and script

- Do not replace a community's language with decorative pseudo-script.
- Verify translations, orthography, direction, and font support.
- Keep the primary interface readable even when another script appears decoratively.
- Do not assume Simplified Chinese is appropriate for every region, period, or audience.

## Festivals and rituals

- Distinguish national public holidays, regional festivals, religious observances, family rituals, tourism events, and contemporary commercial campaigns.
- Check colors, offerings, clothing, food, music, taboos, and calendar conventions in the relevant context.
- Avoid combining wedding, funerary, ancestral, religious, and New Year symbols solely because they appear “traditional.”

## Living crafts

- Name the craft, place, material, technique, and practitioner community when known.
- Do not imply that a generic SVG pattern reproduces a protected, transmitted, or community-specific craft.
- Credit sources and makers when using documented objects as direct visual references.
- Treat contemporary practitioners as present-day creators, not anonymous remnants of the past.

## Default fallback

If reliable context is unavailable, use Han's general contemporary themes with neutral language. Do not invent region-specific or ethnic authenticity.
