## Source quality

Prefer:

1. collection records for a specific object;
2. heritage institutions and standards;
3. peer-reviewed or university publications;
4. catalogues and specialist books;
5. reputable educational summaries.

Use commercial mood boards, stock sites, social posts, and unsourced AI output only for visual discovery. Do not use them as evidence for historical or cultural claims.
